Welcome to Savanna Table

Welcome to Savanna Table, a restaurant inspired by the rich food culture of The Gambia and West Africa. We offer a welcoming place where friends and families can enjoy good food together.

Our restaurant serves freshly prepared meals using familiar local ingredients. From traditional rice dishes to grilled chicken, we aim to give every guest a satisfying dining experience.

Why Visit Us?

Savanna Table is a great place for family meals, friendly gatherings and anyone who wants to enjoy delicious West African food in a relaxed atmosphere.

  • Freshly prepared meals
  • Traditional Gambian flavours
  • A welcoming atmosphere
