Our Location

Savanna Table is located in Serrekunda, The Gambia. It is a welcoming place for residents and visitors who want to enjoy local food.

Our fictional address is Market Street, Serrekunda, The Gambia. Customers can use nearby shops and landmarks to help find the restaurant.

Opening Hours

  • Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • Saturday: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Sunday: 12:00 PM - 8:00 PM

How to Find Us

  1. Travel towards the centre of Serrekunda.
  2. Look for Market Street.
  3. Follow the street until you reach the restaurant.

To learn about The Gambia, visit the official tourism website.
